2PC failure demo - cloud-db killed mid-commit
===============================================
BEFORE:
  core-db:  COMMITTED=129  ABORTED=3
  cloud-db: COMMITTED=129

DURING cloud-db outage:
  core-db:  COMMITTED=346  ABORTED=18  PENDING=1
  Leader (core-1): commitCount=289 abortCount=15
  Logs: "[core:core-1] LEADER #N pid=X risk=0.Y txn=ABORT"
  Delta: +15 ABORTED, +0 COMMITTED - no partial writes leaked through

AFTER cloud-db restored (8s):
  core-db:  COMMITTED=351  ABORTED=20
  cloud-db: COMMITTED=351
  Matching counts again - atomicity held across the outage

Interpretation:
- When cloud-db PREPARE failed, core-db's prepared row was ROLLED BACK
- No txn ever committed on one side only
- System recovered automatically once cloud-db came back
